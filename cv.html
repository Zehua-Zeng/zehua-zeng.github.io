From Data to Decisions

I build intelligent software systems that help people make sense of complex data. My work combines full-stack software engineering, interactive visualization, machine learning, and scalable data systems to create intuitive tools that support exploration, analysis, and decision-making. I enjoy taking ideas from research prototypes to production-ready applications, bridging human-centered design with practical engineering. My work has been recognized at premier visualization venues and has contributed to both academic research and industrial products.
